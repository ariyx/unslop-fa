# Analytical prose rules

Use for essays, reports, explainers, and evidence-driven analysis.

## A01 — Grandiose framing

**Detect:** The opening presents the topic as «یکی از مهم‌ترین تحولات»، «نقطه عطف»، or another sweeping historical claim without evidence.

**Rewrite:** Begin with the observable change, data, or question.

## A02 — Rhetorical question scaffold

**Detect:** Repeated «سؤال اصلی این نیست که... بلکه...» or self-answered questions used mainly to structure the article.

**Rewrite:** Convert to a direct analytical claim unless the question genuinely frames uncertainty.

## A03 — Evidence followed by generic moral

**Detect:** A paragraph gives useful data, then ends with a broad interpretive sentence that adds no analysis.

**Rewrite:** End on the implication that follows specifically from the evidence.

## A04 — Mechanical section thesis

**Detect:** Every section starts with a neat thesis, then explanation, then mini-conclusion.

**Rewrite:** Allow some sections to begin with evidence, a case, a limitation, or a concrete example.
