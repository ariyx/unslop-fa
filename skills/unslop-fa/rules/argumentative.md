# Argumentative prose rules

Use for balanced essays, opinion analysis, and compare-both-sides writing.

## G01 — Forced balance

**Detect:** Every option receives a matching advantage and disadvantage even when the evidence is not symmetrical.

**Rewrite:** Preserve real counterarguments but do not manufacture equal weight.

## G02 — Exhaustive coverage

**Detect:** The draft tries to mention every adjacent option or subtopic at similar length, producing breadth without prioritization.

**Rewrite:** Keep the distinctions that change the conclusion; compress peripheral branches.

## G03 — Safe "it depends" conclusion

**Detect:** The conclusion retreats to «برای همه یکسان نیست» or «بستگی به شرایط دارد» without naming which conditions actually change the answer.

**Rewrite:** State the decision variables concretely.

## G04 — False-dichotomy resolution template

**Detect:** «نباید مسئله را به شکل X در برابر Y دید» followed by a generic compromise.

**Rewrite:** State the interaction or overlap directly.
