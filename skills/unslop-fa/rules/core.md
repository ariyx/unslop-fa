# Core rules

These rules apply across genres. A pattern is not slop merely because it appears. Flag it only when it is repeated, unnecessary, generic, or harmful to clarity or voice.

Each rule has four parts: **Detect**, **Do not flag**, **Rewrite**, and **Example**.

## C01 — Semantic repetition

**Detect:** Two or more nearby sentences or sections communicate the same idea with different wording but add no new fact, example, qualification, or consequence.

**Do not flag:** Deliberate repetition used for contrast, instruction, emphasis, or narrative callback when each recurrence changes the reader's understanding, advances chronology, or marks a meaningful before/after.

**Rewrite:** Keep the strongest formulation. Merge the rest or replace repetition with a concrete detail.

**Example:**

Before: «همه اطلاعات در یک محیط مشترک قرار می‌گیرند. اعضای تیم نیز به یک منبع واحد از اطلاعات دسترسی دارند.»

After: «وظایف، فایل‌ها و وضعیت پروژه در یک فضای مشترک قرار می‌گیرند.»

## C02 — Redundant restatement

**Detect:** A sentence announces «به عبارت دیگر»، «یعنی»، «در واقع» or an equivalent move and then restates the previous sentence without clarifying it.

**Do not flag:** A restatement that genuinely simplifies a technical point, defines a term, or removes ambiguity.

**Rewrite:** Delete the gloss or keep only the clearer version.

## C03 — Formulaic binary contrast

**Detect:** Repeated frames such as «مسئله X نیست، بلکه Y است»، «فقط X نیست»، or «سؤال اصلی این نیست که...». The contrast sounds rhetorical rather than informative.

**Do not flag:** A real distinction where both sides matter to the reasoning.

**Rewrite:** State the stronger claim directly or express the contrast in ordinary syntax.

## C04 — Excessive symmetry

**Detect:** Repeated three-part lists, mirrored clauses, paired oppositions, or paragraphs with nearly identical internal structure.

**Do not flag:** Taxonomies, comparisons, procedures, or technical lists where symmetry improves comprehension.

**Rewrite:** Break only the ornamental symmetry. Preserve structure that carries information.

## C05 — Canned transition

**Detect:** Repeated paragraph openers such as «بنابراین»، «در نتیجه»، «از این منظر»، «در مقابل»، «به همین دلیل»، or «در چنین شرایطی» when the relation is already obvious.

**Do not flag:** A connector that prevents a real logical jump.

**Rewrite:** Remove the connector, join the sentences, or state the causal relation directly.

## C06 — Generic abstraction

**Detect:** Claims built from broad labels such as «تحول بزرگ»، «نقش مهم»، «فرصت گسترده»، «چالش جدی»، «تأثیر قابل‌توجه» without saying what changed, for whom, or how.

**Do not flag:** A brief summary that is immediately supported by specifics.

**Rewrite:** Prefer the concrete mechanism, consequence, number, example, or decision already present in the source.

## C07 — Portability filler

**Detect:** A sentence could be moved unchanged to many unrelated people, companies, products, or topics.

**Do not flag:** Necessary framing or definitions that are generic by nature. In personal or educational prose, do not flag a broad sentence merely because it could apply elsewhere when it is clearly part of the writer's established voice, motivation, or teaching rhythm.

**Rewrite:** Cut it or make it specific using facts already in the draft. Never invent specificity.

## C08 — Interpretive metacommentary

**Detect:** The prose tells the reader how important, surprising, obvious, or meaningful a point is instead of showing why.

Examples include «این نکته بسیار مهم است»، «این تفاوت اهمیت زیادی دارد»، or repeated «نکته اصلی این است».

**Do not flag:** Signposting needed in long technical or legal prose, or a first-person judgment in a review or personal piece where the evaluation itself is part of the writer's voice.

**Rewrite:** Delete the label or replace it with the evidence or consequence.

## C09 — Robotic rhythm

**Detect:** Repeated sentence lengths, repeated paragraph templates, stacked short punchlines, or a sequence that sounds mechanically balanced.

**Do not flag:** Deliberate rhythm that is clearly part of the writer's voice, including colloquial repetition, uneven sentence lengths, and isolated fragments in personal prose.

**Rewrite:** Change only enough sentence shape to restore natural cadence. Do not make every sentence the same length in the opposite direction.

## C10 — Aphoristic or fake-profound ending

**Detect:** The final line turns the piece into a slogan, universal lesson, or quotable opposition without adding a supported point.

**Do not flag:** A distinctive closing line that clearly belongs to the writer and is grounded in the piece.

**Rewrite:** End on the clearest concrete implication, decision, limitation, or next action already supported by the draft.

## C11 — Summary-recap ending

**Detect:** The conclusion walks through the article again using the same vocabulary and structure.

**Do not flag:** A required executive summary, academic abstract, or conclusion format.

**Rewrite:** Compress the recap and keep the final synthesis or consequence.

## C12 — Vague attribution

**Detect:** «کارشناسان معتقدند»، «تحقیقات نشان می‌دهد»، «گزارش‌ها حاکی از آن است»، or similar authority claims without a source.

**Do not flag:** The source is named elsewhere and the reference is unambiguous.

**Rewrite:** Name the source if provided. Otherwise remove the borrowed authority or flag the missing source. Never invent one.
