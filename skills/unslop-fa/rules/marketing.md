# Marketing and product-copy rules

Use for landing pages, product descriptions, launch copy, and corporate marketing prose.

## M01 — Triple-benefit slogan

**Detect:** Headings and CTAs built from three interchangeable positive adjectives or verbs: «ساده‌تر، هوشمندتر، سریع‌تر».

**Rewrite:** Prefer the product's concrete capability or strongest differentiator.

## M02 — Template problem-solution opener

**Detect:** A broad pain-point sentence followed immediately by «X برای حل همین مشکل طراحی شده است» without showing what the product actually does.

**Rewrite:** Lead with the specific workflow or constraint the product changes.

## M03 — Abstract benefit language

**Detect:** «شفافیت بیشتر»، «تمرکز بالاتر»، «هماهنگی بهتر»، «تصمیم‌گیری سریع‌تر» without observable behavior or support.

**Rewrite:** Describe what the user can see, do, avoid, or measure.

## M04 — Feature → obvious benefit cadence

**Detect:** Every section follows the same sequence: feature, explanation, generic benefit sentence.

**Rewrite:** Vary only where useful; merge the generic benefit into the feature description.

## M05 — Repeated value proposition

**Detect:** Multiple sections restate «everything in one place» or another core benefit with different vocabulary.

**Rewrite:** State the benefit once, then let later sections explain distinct capabilities.

## M06 — Universal-fit claim

**Detect:** «فرقی نمی‌کند تیم کوچک باشید یا سازمان بزرگ...» when the draft provides no evidence for the full range.

**Rewrite:** Name the actual target audience or supported use cases.

## M07 — CTA recap

**Detect:** The final CTA repeats three or more benefits already stated in the page.

**Rewrite:** Use one clear action and, if needed, one concrete reason to take it.
