# Social and personal-post rules

Use for LinkedIn posts, personal reflections, threads, captions, and professional social writing.

## S01 — Dramatic fragment stack

**Detect:** Repeated one-line fragments used to manufacture emotional rhythm: «نشد.» «همین.» «تمام.»

**Do not flag:** A rare fragment that clearly matches the writer's normal voice.

**Rewrite:** Merge into surrounding prose or keep only the strongest fragment.

## S02 — Negative-list setup

**Detect:** «نه به این دلیل که X. نه به این دلیل که Y.» followed by the reveal.

**Rewrite:** State the actual cause directly, unless the contrast carries real narrative tension.

## S03 — Lesson stack

**Detect:** A sequence of polished maxims presented as lessons, each designed to stand alone as a quote.

**Rewrite:** Keep the concrete lessons and connect them to what happened in the story.

## S04 — Manufactured personal revelation

**Detect:** Stock turns such as «امروز که به گذشته نگاه می‌کنم» or «این تجربه نگاه من را تغییر داد» when no specific change follows.

**Rewrite:** Name what the writer does differently now.

## S05 — Engagement-bait ending

**Detect:** One or more generic audience questions appended solely to trigger comments.

**Do not flag:** A genuine question that invites relevant experience or is normal for the platform.

**Rewrite:** Keep at most the question that advances the discussion.

## S06 — Hashtag tail

**Detect:** A long generic block of broad hashtags unrelated to discoverability or audience conventions.

**Rewrite:** Remove or reduce only when the user wants tighter copy. Do not assume hashtags are always wrong.
