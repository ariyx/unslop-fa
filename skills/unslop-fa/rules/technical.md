# Technical prose rules and safeguards

Technical editing should be conservative. Precision outranks stylistic variety.

## T01 — Preserve caveats

Do not remove qualifiers that limit scope, population, benchmark, workload, environment, or confidence.

Example: «این نتیجه را نمی‌توان به همه پروژه‌ها تعمیم داد» may look formulaic but carries technical meaning.

## T02 — Preserve terminology

Do not rotate correct technical terms merely to avoid repetition. Consistent naming is often better than stylistic synonym cycling.

## T03 — Preserve numbers and source attribution

Never change a number, unit, date, benchmark result, named source, API name, or version during unslop editing unless the user explicitly asks for factual correction.

## T04 — Do not simplify informative comparisons

A sentence such as «مقایسه performance بدون مشخص کردن workload معنی‌دار نیست» is substantive, not filler, if the surrounding comparison depends on workload.

## T05 — Examples must earn their place

Keep examples that explain a mechanism or boundary. Remove only examples that repeat an already clear point without adding technical understanding.

## T06 — Prefer stable technical wording over forced Persianization

Keep conventional English technical terms when translation would reduce clarity for the target audience. Avoid gratuitous mixing when a standard Persian term is clearer.
