# analytical-001 — Detect baseline

Genre: analytical + technical

This baseline records the main observable patterns that should be discoverable without making any claim about authorship.

| Rule | Passage | Why it is a problem here | Revision direction |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A01 — Grandiose framing | «برنامه‌نویسی را وارد یکی از مهم‌ترین دوره‌های تحول خود کرده است» | The opening makes a sweeping historical claim before showing the concrete change. | Start with what the tools can now do and let the significance follow from the evidence. |
| A02 — Rhetorical question scaffold | «پرسش اصلی دیگر این نیست که... پرسش مهم‌تر این است که...» | The article uses a self-answered question to organize a point that can be stated directly. | State the actual analytical question or claim without the staged reframe. |
| C03 — Formulaic binary contrast | «مزیت اصلی توسعه‌دهنده دیگر حفظ APIها نیست؛ بلکه...» and later «مسئله اصلی احتمالاً «حذف برنامه‌نویسی» نیست، بلکه...» | The repeated «X نیست، بلکه Y است» frame becomes a visible template across sections. | Keep the underlying distinction but vary the syntax and state the stronger claim directly. |
| C04 — Excessive symmetry | «نخستین مهارت مهم...»، «دومین مهارت...»، «در کنار آن...»، «مهارت مهم دیگر...»، «در نهایت...» | The skills section is mechanically sequenced even though the points do not require a numbered rhetorical ladder. | Keep all skills, but remove ornamental ordering and let each paragraph begin from its content. |
| C05 — Canned transition | Repeated «به همین دلیل»، «در چنین محیطی»، «به بیان دیگر»، «بنابراین»، «در نتیجه»، «در نهایت» | Several connectors announce relationships that are already clear from adjacent sentences. | Remove or replace only the redundant connectors; keep those that carry real logic. |
| P05 — Habitual modal stacking | Repeated «احتمالاً»، «می‌تواند»، «ممکن است» across forecasts and ordinary descriptive sentences | Some uncertainty is substantive, but the density of modal wording gives unrelated claims the same hedged cadence. | Preserve uncertainty around forecasts and evidence limits; make ordinary descriptive claims direct where the source already supports them. |
| C11 — Summary-recap ending | The conclusion repeats routine coding, lower-value skills, higher-value skills, and labor-market change in the same sequence used earlier. | The ending spends several paragraphs retelling the article before reaching its synthesis. | Compress the recap and keep the strongest supported implication. |
| C10 — Aphoristic or fake-profound ending | «آینده متعلق به کسانی نیست که... مزیت اصلی متعلق به توسعه‌دهندگانی خواهد بود که...» | The final sentence turns the analysis into a broad, quote-ready maxim. | End on the concrete engineering shift already supported by the article: problem definition, system design, and validation of AI output. |

## Safeguards

The edit must preserve:

- all named sources and dates;
- all reported percentages and counts, including `۸۴ درصد`, `۵۱ درصد`, `۴۶ درصد`, `۳۳ درصد`, `۱۹ درصد`, `۱۵٫۸ درصد`, and `۲۶۷٬۷۰۰`;
- the scope limitation on the METR study;
- the limitation that U.S. labor-market data should not be generalized directly to every country;
- technical terms and distinctions such as API, AI, testing, security, architecture, and system design.
