# marketing-001 — Detect baseline

Genre: marketing / product landing page

This baseline records generic SaaS-copy patterns without treating ordinary persuasive writing as evidence of AI authorship.

| Rule | Passage | Why it is a problem here | Revision direction |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| M01 — Triple-benefit slogan | Title: «مدیریت پروژه، ساده‌تر، هوشمندتر و هماهنگ‌تر» and the final three-part CTA | The three interchangeable positive benefits sound polished but do not tell the reader what FlowTask actually changes in the workflow. | Lead with the concrete product function or strongest differentiator. |
| M02 — Template problem-solution opener | «مدیریت پروژه زمانی پیچیده می‌شود که...» followed by «FlowTask برای حل همین مسئله طراحی شده است» | The opener follows a generic pain → product-solution template before explaining the actual workflow. | Introduce FlowTask through the specific problem it consolidates: tasks, deadlines, files, and project-status reporting. |
| M03 — Abstract benefit language | «شفافیت بیشتر»، «تمرکز بالاتر»، «تصمیم‌های سریع‌تر و دقیق‌تر»، «هماهنگی ساده‌تر» | These benefits are plausible but mostly unobservable and repeated without concrete support. | Replace them with visible product behavior: who owns a task, what is late, where a file lives, what remains incomplete. |
| M04 — Feature → obvious benefit cadence | Most sections describe a feature and then close with a generic benefit sentence | Repeating the same section rhythm makes the page predictable and lengthens it without adding product information. | Let the capability and example carry the benefit; remove generic summary sentences when they add no new information. |
| M05 — Repeated value proposition | «همه اطلاعات ... در یک مکان»، «محیط مشترک»، «یک منبع مشترک از اطلاعات»، «یک فضای کاری واحد» | The same “everything in one place” value proposition appears in several sections with different wording. | State consolidation once, then use later sections for distinct capabilities. |
| C01 — Semantic repetition | Task visibility, team coordination, and the final “single workspace” section repeatedly restate the same visibility/coordination benefit | The wording changes, but little new information is introduced in the later repetitions. | Merge overlapping claims and keep the most concrete formulation. |
| C03 — Formulaic binary contrast | «FlowTask فقط یک ابزار ثبت وظایف نیست.» | The «فقط X نیست» frame is a generic rhetorical upgrade and does not itself clarify the product. | Replace it with the additional capabilities directly. |
| M06 — Universal-fit claim | «فرقی نمی‌کند یک تیم کوچک ... یا یک مجموعه بزرگ...» | The copy claims suitability across very different organization sizes without evidence or specific enterprise capabilities. | Describe the target workflow rather than claiming universal fit. |
| M07 — CTA recap | «پروژه‌ها را با دید بهتر مدیریت کنید، تیم خود را هماهنگ نگه دارید و بدون اتلاف وقت...» | The CTA restates several abstract benefits already repeated throughout the page. | End with one concrete action or one concise product summary. |

## Safeguards

The edit must preserve the capabilities actually stated in the source:

- creating, categorizing, prioritizing, and assigning a `task`;
- setting and tracking a `deadline`;
- associating files and documents with project work;
- giving team members access to current task/project status;
- generating project-progress reports with `هوش مصنوعی` from existing project information;
- the product name `FlowTask`;
- the absence of invented metrics, integrations, automations, pricing, notifications, dashboards, or enterprise features not stated in the source.

## Non-findings

The following should not be removed merely because they are common product-copy structures:

- section headings that help scanning;
- the concrete explanation of what the AI-generated progress report contains;
- the explanation that files can be kept next to related project work;
- references to task ownership and due dates, because those are actual product capabilities rather than abstract benefits.
