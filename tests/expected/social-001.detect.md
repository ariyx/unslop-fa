# social-001 — Detect baseline

Genre: social / professional personal post

This baseline records the main observable patterns that should be discoverable without making any claim about authorship.

| Rule | Passage | Why it is a problem here | Revision direction |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| S02 — Negative-list setup | «نه به این دلیل که تیم ضعیفی داشتیم. نه به این دلیل که کسی تلاش نمی‌کرد.» | The repeated negation delays the actual cause and creates a staged reveal. | State the actual problem earlier, while preserving the useful contrast that the team was capable and worked hard. |
| C09 — Robotic rhythm | Standalone lines such as «یکی از بزرگ‌ترین اشتباهات ما...»، «اشتباه دوم...»، «بین این دو تفاوت بزرگی وجود دارد.» and especially «نشد.» | The post repeatedly alternates medium paragraphs with short punchlines, producing a mechanically dramatic cadence. | Merge ornamental fragments into surrounding prose and keep only breaks that reflect a real shift in the story. |
| S04 — Manufactured personal revelation | «اما امروز که به آن دوره نگاه می‌کنم...» and «این تجربه برای من چند درس مهم داشت» | These transitions announce reflection before giving the concrete insight. | Move directly to what the writer now believes or does differently. |
| S03 — Lesson stack | «سادگی یک مزیت فنی است...»، «کد خوب...»، «ارتباط با مشتری...»، «و شاید مهم‌تر از همه...» | Four polished maxims are stacked as quotable lessons, detached from the specific events that produced them. | Keep the lessons but reconnect them to the architecture, customer-discovery, and sunk-cost mistakes described earlier. |
| C10 — Aphoristic or fake-profound ending | «آیا اصلاً باید این را بسازیم؟» is staged as the final revelation after a separate lead-in | The insight is relevant, but the isolated question is framed as a slogan-like final turn. | State the changed decision habit directly or keep the question inline rather than staging it as a reveal. |
| S05 — Engagement-bait ending | Two consecutive audience questions: «برای شما هم پیش آمده...؟» and «اگر به گذشته برگردید...؟» | One question can invite a useful discussion; two generic prompts read like an engagement template. | Keep at most the more specific question. |
| S06 — Hashtag tail | `#SoftwareEngineering #Programming #LessonsLearned #SoftwareDevelopment #ProductDevelopment #Teamwork` | The broad six-tag block is generic and adds little to the content. | Remove it in a tighter editorial baseline; if platform discoverability matters, retain only a small number of targeted tags. |
| S01 — Dramatic fragment stack | «نشد.» and the isolated «آیا اصلاً باید این را بسازیم؟» | The fragments are used as dramatic beats rather than because the syntax requires them. | Integrate them into surrounding sentences unless the writer explicitly prefers that punchy style. |

## Safeguards

The edit must preserve:

- the personal first-person perspective;
- the two concrete failure modes: `پیچیده کردن زودهنگام سیستم` and `ارتباط ضعیف با مشتری`;
- the distinction between working hard and working on the right problem;
- the concrete examples of premature architecture, weak customer discovery, delayed concerns, and sunk-cost behavior;
- the writer's changed habit of asking whether something should be built before asking how to build it;
- a professional social-post tone rather than converting the post into an essay or report.
